Lyra Tune 0.6.7 - VST3 + Audio Unit, macOS 11 or later (Apple Silicon and Intel)

Install:
  VST3 (Ableton Live, FL Studio, Reaper, Cubase, Studio One, Bitwig...):
    copy "Lyra Tune.vst3"      to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
  Audio Unit (Logic Pro, GarageBand, also most VST3 hosts):
    copy "Lyra Tune.component" to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
  then rescan plugins in your DAW (Logic: restart it; it validates new AUs on launch).

First launch:
  The plugin is signed ad-hoc but not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it after download.
  Allow it once in Terminal:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"Lyra Tune.vst3"
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"Lyra Tune.component"
  or allow it in System Settings > Privacy & Security after the first warning.

Added latency: 3.6 ms (Range High), 5.3 ms (Mid), 7.4 ms (Low) at 48 kHz, +2 ms with HQ on; reported to the host.
