Lyra Tune 0.6.7 - VST3 + Audio Unit, macOS 11 or later (Apple Silicon and Intel)

Install:
  1. Copy "Lyra Tune.component" (Audio Unit) to  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
  2. Copy "Lyra Tune.vst3"                     to  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
  3. The plugin is not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time.
     Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Allow Anyway"
     ("Open Anyway" on some macOS versions) - once for the VST3 and once for the component.
  4. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins. In FL Studio, add Lyra Tune to an effect slot in the Mixer.

  Alternative to step 3, in Terminal:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"Lyra Tune.vst3"
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"Lyra Tune.component"

Added latency: 3.6 ms (Range High), 5.3 ms (Mid), 7.4 ms (Low) at 48 kHz, +2 ms with HQ on; reported to the host.
